## Discovery and your library

- Radar brings together For You picks, weekly trends, popular titles, and release calendars. Digital Releases shows verified Egyptian dates from TMDB.
- Following lets you track actors and directors by role and browse their recent and upcoming work.
- Global Search highlights top matches and remembers recent queries. My Library has clearer filtering, sorting, and empty states.
- Metadata caching reuses work across discovery, detail, and library views.

## Appearance

- Choose Default, Project Hail Mary, or La La Land. Each gallery shows actual screenshots of the built-in layouts.
- La La Land brings indigo surfaces, serif titles, and a floating bottom dock. Rearrange, hide, or restore destinations directly from the dock.
- Theme Studio supports live editing and custom `.framestyle` imports and exports. Recently Added keeps background images available across restarts.
- Older selections of Dune, Doomsday, or Zodiac fall back to Default. Saved custom themes are preserved.
