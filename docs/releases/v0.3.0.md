Frame 0.3.0 brings a new desktop interface for browsing, organizing and watching movies and TV.

- A compact navigation rail and command bar bring search, back and forward navigation, and your profile into reach.
- Home pairs its feature with a selectable title index and gives Continue Watching a dedicated area. Discover uses separate spaces for the artwork, synopsis and stills.
- Movie and TV pages put the title, playback actions and synopsis next to the artwork. Episode lists and credits follow below.
- My Library puts collections beside the filters and results. Settings shows pages by category and still searches across all settings.
- Search, menus, dialogs and playback controls share clearer layouts. Featured carousels pause while focused and respect reduced motion.
- The new layouts apply to Default and AMOLED. Imported `.framestyle` themes keep their own layouts, and existing settings and library data remain compatible.

The Windows installer includes the Capstan runtime for supported Android extensions. Radar and Following require a TMDB key. Radar's Digital Releases feed is still unavailable.
